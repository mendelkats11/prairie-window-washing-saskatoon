FAQ

Solar Panel Cleaning questions, answered

How much can dirty solar panels affect energy output?

Studies commonly show a 5-25% drop in output from heavily soiled panels, depending on buildup and local conditions. Regular cleaning helps keep your system performing closer to its rated capacity.

How often should solar panels be cleaned in Saskatchewan?

Most homeowners see good results with one to two cleanings per year, though homes near gravel roads or with heavy bird activity may benefit from more frequent visits.

Is it safe to walk on my roof to clean panels?

Our crew uses proper safety equipment and takes care to avoid stepping directly on panels or their mounting hardware.
