FAQ

Window Washing questions, answered

How much does window washing cost in Saskatoon?

Pricing depends on the number of panes, the number of storeys, and whether you'd like interior glass included. Send us a few details through our quote form and we'll follow up with an honest, up-front price.

How often should I get my windows professionally cleaned?

Most Saskatoon homeowners book a professional clean twice a year — once in spring after the snow melts and again in fall before winter sets in. Commercial properties often go quarterly or monthly.

Do you clean windows in the winter?

We recommend scheduling window washing above freezing temperatures, roughly April through October in Saskatoon. We're happy to work around mild winter days when conditions allow.

Is the water you use safe for my siding, plants and pets?

Yes. We use purified water and, where needed, eco-friendly, biodegradable solutions that are safe for lawns, gardens and pets.
