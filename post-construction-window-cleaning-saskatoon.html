FAQ

Post-Construction Cleaning questions, answered

When should post-construction window cleaning happen?

Ideally once interior and exterior construction dust has settled but before final walkthroughs — we can coordinate timing with your build schedule.

Can you remove dried paint or stucco overspray?

In most cases, yes. We use techniques and tools suited to glass that safely lift dried overspray without scratching.

Do you work with builders and contractors directly?

Yes — we regularly coordinate directly with builders and project managers on new-build and renovation timelines.
